You are part of an AI consultancy group contracted by IntelliSys Ltd., an established Intelligent Systems Company that provides sensor-driven and software-based solutions to clients in a variety of sectors, including healthcare, finance, environmental monitoring, transportation and mobility, agriculture, and digital media/language processing. Although IntelliSys has extensive domain knowledge and system integration expertise, it currently lacks the in-house capability to integrate Artificial Intelligence (AI) and Deep Learning (DL) into its services.

To address this gap, IntelliSys has commissioned your consultancy team to explore and demonstrate how deep learning can enhance its offerings. Your team’s goal is to design a high-quality prototype and provide practical recommendations on how IntelliSys can adopt AI responsibly and effectively within one of its client domains.

To meet the client’s expectations, your consultancy team must:
•	Identify, select, and discuss a specific real-world problem relevant to one of IntelliSys’s client sectors and propose a deep learning-based approach (design, implement, and evaluate an appropriate DL model) that your group would recommend for solving it. Clearly explain your design decisions and evaluate your model performance. Also, include a discussion on interpretability to demonstrate transparency and ethical awareness.
•	Provide actionable technical recommendations to help IntelliSys Ltd. extend its services by adopting AI capabilities, discuss potential risks or limitations of your deployed solution and propose potential mitigating solutions.

To support your work, you may select datasets provided on Moodle or propose an alternative dataset relevant to your chosen sector (subject to approval). You are encouraged to be creative and thoughtful in your solution design, while maintaining rigour in your technical work and clarity in your client-facing communication.

Your group must demonstrate collaborative working by coordinating effectively as a team to divide responsibilities and integrate your contributions. Ensure that each group member contributes meaningfully to the structure and usability of the code, and the presentation delivery. Ensure your communication is well-targeted to both technical and non-technical audiences and shows professionalism in how the group manages the consultancy brief.

Your final deliverable will be a 15-minute group presentation supported by professional-quality slides and a well-documented and reproducible Jupyter notebook, delivered to an audience that includes both peers and tutors acting as company stakeholders. You are expected to demonstrate technical proficiency, critical thinking, teamwork, and effective communication throughout.

References from both academic and commercial sources should be included. Ensure your presentation is clear, objective, and well-structured, adhering to academic standards.

Additional documents will be provided during the module on Moodle, as well as an FAQ.

You can use AI tools in your workflow. However, your application must clearly reflect your own reasoning and decision-making, critical evaluation, original coding effort, design and implementation decisions. Although AI assistance is allowed during code development, you must adapt and extend any AI-generated content to reflect your understanding and ownership of the work. This aims to bridge applied AI development with responsible practice, ethical reasoning, and awareness of industry and research trends.
